02 / Flight system

Built in the browser

The scene is generated at runtime: 720 instanced cloud billboards, a procedural sky shader, and a hand-built aviator with animated scarf, wings and jet exhaust. The camera banks with path curvature, then hands control to you the moment you touch the keys.

Renderer
Three.js r160, ACES tone mapping, UnrealBloom, WebGL2
Flight model
Closed Catmull-Rom autopilot; manual yaw, pitch, throttle and bank
Clouds
720 camera-facing instanced quads with a canvas-generated texture
Controls
A/D bank, W/S throttle, arrows pitch, M mode, L light, P 4K still
Export
3840 × 2160 PNG captured from the live composer
Dependencies
Vendored locally. No network calls while flying.
